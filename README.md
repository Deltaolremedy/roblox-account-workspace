## 💡 What Is This?

**Roblox Account Workspace** is your all-in-one dashboard for managing multiple Roblox profiles. Whether you're a player, developer, collector, or content creator — this extension gives you total control over your accounts in one place.

> 🚀 **Switch accounts in 1 second** • 🔐 **Bank-level security** • 🌐 **Works on all browsers**
